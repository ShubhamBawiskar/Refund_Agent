# E-Commerce Refund Policy

Welcome to our automated customer support. We are happy to process returns for eligible items. Please adhere to the following strict refund policies:

**Rule 1: Time Limit**
No refunds will be granted for orders placed more than 30 days ago.

**Rule 2: Final Sale Items**
Any item marked as "Final Sale" is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

**Rule 3: High-Value Items**
Refunds for items valued over $500 cannot be processed automatically by the AI agent and must be escalated to a human agent.

If a customer's request violates Rule 2 or Rule 3, please inform them that the refund cannot be processed automatically and provide the appropriate reason based on the rules above.
